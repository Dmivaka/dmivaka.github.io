# About me

I design embedded electronics for robots.

My work covers the complete path from system architecture and PCB design to STM32 firmware, motor control, communication, hardware bring-up, and low-volume production. Over 7+ years I have developed electronics for quadrupeds, educational robots, mobile research platforms, and reusable robotic hardware.

Please refer to [portfolio](/portfolio/) for detailed information.
